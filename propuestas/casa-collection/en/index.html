01 · The brief

Four businesses on a website built for one.

Holiday rentals in Costa Adeje, Los Cristianos, Palm Mar and Callao Salvaje. Experiences, from boat trips to the Grand Tour of Teide. Real estate. And a fourth line, the one with the most future and the least visibility today: Owners, your B2B business.

That's four different audiences with four different journeys. Today they share a structure that doesn't tell them apart, and visitors have to work out for themselves which of the four they're in.

You asked for a more professional image, simple navigation and direct bookings. And a fourth thing, the one that shapes the technical design most: you want to manage it yourselves, with our support behind you when you need it.

Your own photography. The material is there, and it's good.

02 · What we found on your site

Before writing this, we studied it.

It isn't a badly made website. It's a website you've outgrown: it's built on a page builder, and that kind of tool holds up well for one business and starts to creak with four. This is what we found.

Platform
Duda, a page builder. It explains today's limits well: there's no dashboard designed for a portfolio of properties, and every new listing is manual work instead of a record.
Languages
Spanish, English and French. The translation is incomplete: the English version mixes labels in both languages. An English or French visitor who sees a half-translated site starts doubting the rest.
Booking accommodation
The "Choose your dates" search works, but it takes the guest off your domain to finish the booking. The design changes, the address changes, and right at the moment of paying the guest is no longer in your house. That's where people drop off.
Booking experiences
Nine experiences published, with their prices, and all nine with a button that says "Book this experience". None of them books anything: the icon on all nine buttons is WhatsApp's.
Social proof
The four reviews on the home page quote Airbnb and Booking.com. Your reputation is built, but it's borrowed: it lives on the platforms, not on your domain.
Smoobu
It's credited in the footer. Good news: you've already solved the hard part, which is calendar sync. What's missing is connecting it to a website that matches it.

03 · The gap

Nine buttons that say "Book" and open WhatsApp.

Smoobu handles accommodation, not activities. That's why rentals work and experiences don't: there's no tool behind them, just a person answering messages.

This is what you have published today, with your prices and your photography:

Prices "from", taken from your website on 7 November 2026.

A guest already staying with you is the easiest customer you'll ever have: they've already paid, they're already on the island and they want to spend more. But they decide at eleven at night, from the villa sofa, and at that moment nobody is answering WhatsApp.

The experiences booking engine is included in this proposal, not an extra. It's the difference between refreshing a website and adding a revenue line that takes payments on its own, at night and in three languages.

04 · What we build

One website, one dashboard and two booking engines.

The website

Custom design, not a template. Four clear journeys from the home page: holiday rentals, experiences, real estate and owners. Listings with gallery, map and features. A sales search with filters. Spanish, English and French, properly translated.

The dashboard

Yours, with no technical knowledge needed. Add and remove properties, accommodation listings, experiences with their times and capacity, and the text, photos and prices of any page. Handed over with training and a recording.

Accommodation bookings

Built on your current Smoobu, but properly integrated: guests search, choose and pay without ever leaving your domain. Smoobu stays the only owner of the calendar, so your sync with Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo stays exactly as it is.

Experience bookings

Our own engine, because Smoobu doesn't cover them. A calendar per activity, capacity, time slots, seasons, adult and child prices, card payment and automatic email confirmation.

05 · Your questions

Straight answers.

Do you work with WordPress or another platform?

Yes, we work with WordPress when a project calls for it. For yours we don't recommend it.

Your case has four lines of business, property listings, bookings and payments. In WordPress that ends up as six or seven third-party plugins that update on their own; the day one of them breaks the calendar, the fix isn't in our hands. And along the way you'd have swapped one page builder for another system you don't fully control either.

We build custom websites with our own management dashboard on top: it's faster, there are no plugins to maintain, and whatever breaks, we fix, because we wrote it.

Could we manage the content, photos, prices and properties ourselves?

Yes, and it's the part we care most about getting right. You add and remove properties for sale with their photos, prices and features; edit accommodation listings; create and change experiences with times, capacity and seasons; and change the text, photos and prices of any page on the site. Without touching code and without asking us.

One difference from what you have now: properties and experiences stop being standalone pages and become records. Publishing a new villa will mean filling in a form, not laying out a page.

Do you offer technical support and ongoing maintenance?

Yes, for a monthly fee. It includes managed hosting, SSL certificate and domain; daily backups with restore; security updates and downtime monitoring; up to three hours a month of changes you request; support by email and WhatsApp with a reply within 24 working hours; and a quarterly report on visits and bookings.

Not included, and we say so to avoid surprises either way: new features, new sections and redesigns, which are quoted separately.

Would the booking engine integrate with your current Smoobu?

Yes. We integrate Smoobu's booking engine into the new site with our design on top, so guests book without leaving your website. Smoobu remains the only source of availability and prices: your policies, your payment methods and your sync with the platforms keep working exactly as they do now.

It's a deliberate choice: no system of ours should compete with your channel manager over which days are free.
