Rental agreement & cancellation policy

Reservation. A reservation is confirmed once payment is received. The guest count stated at booking may not be exceeded without written approval.

Cancellation. More than 60 days before arrival, the deposit is refunded in full. Between 30 and 60 days, 50% is refunded. Under 30 days, the reservation is non-refundable.

Check-in and check-out. Check-in from 15:00. Check-out by 11:00. Early arrival or late departure subject to availability.

Damages. The guest is responsible for any damage to the property or its contents during the stay.

House rules. No events or parties without prior written approval. Quiet hours from 22:00. Smoking is not permitted indoors.

This is demonstration text. In production this links to the property's own signed agreement.
