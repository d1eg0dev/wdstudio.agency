Overview

Where the hillside
meets the bay

Villa Escala sits perched on the hillside above Zihuatanejo, completely renovated from top to bottom, with unbroken ocean views from every corner. Six bedrooms, a seventh on request — six king beds and three daybeds, sleeping fifteen comfortably and seventeen with the additional master.

A private infinity pool. A gourmet kitchen with the best view in town, and a custom full-service bar built for a group that has no intention of going out. An expansive lawn for yoga and meditation, fully fenced, with security on site around the clock.

Breakfast and freshly brewed coffee arrive each morning. Housekeeping comes daily. The chef is available whenever you'd rather not think about dinner. Nothing here requires organising.

The area

Playa La Ropa,
Zihuatanejo

Six minutes on foot to Playa La Ropa — the stretch of Pacific sand that has stood in for paradise in film, most famously in the closing scene of The Shawshank Redemption. Twelve minutes to the centre of town, where the markets, the boats and the restaurants are.

  • —Snorkelling, diving and hidden coves by kayak
  • —Deep-sea fishing and yachting from the bay
  • —Whale watching in season
  • —Surfing, ziplining, cycling and the ancient ruins
