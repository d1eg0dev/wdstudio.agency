The villa

The ocean is
the only schedule.

Casa Constantia sits directly on the sand in Puerto Escondido, with five bedrooms, a 23-metre infinity pool and a staff that makes the whole thing feel like it runs itself.

Every bedroom opens onto a private garden and its own bath. Four master suites face the water, close enough that the waves are the first thing you hear. A bunk room holds six more, which is what turns a house into somewhere a family or a group of friends can actually stay.

Nothing here asks for effort. That is the entire point.
