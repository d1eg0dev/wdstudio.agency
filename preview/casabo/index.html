The hotel

A house that
runs on Baja time

Casa Bo sits in a quiet corner of downtown La Paz, one block from the beach. Eleven suites across three buildings — deluxe rooms, studios, and suites with private patios and outdoor bathtubs.

Handmade furniture and lighting. A pool ringed by cactus, sun beds, an outdoor shower. And a rooftop for yoga, a barbecue, or doing nothing at all while the sky works through its colours.
