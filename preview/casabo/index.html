The hotel

Cactus, concrete
and Baja light

Casa Bo sits in a quiet corner of downtown La Paz, one block from the water. Eleven suites across three buildings — deluxe rooms, studios and suites with private patios and outdoor bathtubs.

Handmade furniture, minimalist rooms, a pool ringed by cactus, and a rooftop for yoga, barbecue, or doing nothing at all while Baja works through its colours.
