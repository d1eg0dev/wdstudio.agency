The villa

The whole house,
and nobody else.

Casa Zee sits directly on a quiet beach in Santa María Colotepec, twenty minutes from Puerto Escondido and thirty from the airport. Eight bedrooms, nine bathrooms, room for twenty.

Inside and outside stop being separate. Every principal space faces the water, the pool ends where the view begins, and a full-time staff — including a chef — makes the day run without anyone having to organise it.

Built for multi-generational families, retreats, and groups who would rather have a house than a hotel.
